# High-street unit

Two-storey Victorian high-street building, front +Z, single part `main` (pipeline: raw); the Clearance Zone places shop-a (default) and the pub (`pub`, X 4.5, Z 5.5; also variant `high-street-pub`). Eaves-to-street slate roof on brick gables with stack(s), timber shopfront (shop) or painted timber pub front (pub), sashes above, timber first floor butting the walls. Services: consumer unit, light, boiler, radiator, gas to an outside meter, sink stopcock.

Load paths: roof slabs seat on the front/back wall tops and the gable triangles; the shopfront opening is spanned by a steel bressummer; the pub front is a timber wall with timber lintels.

Pub front: green 'joinery'-finish timber (pale undercoat chips, no red primer bleed), fielded panel stall risers, gilded-capital pilasters, lettered fascia with cornice.

Fittings: the pub has its bar along the blank east gable (a panelled mahogany counter with a polished top and a three-pull beer engine, the servery, the back bar of panelled cupboards under two shelves of bottles in front of a mirror) and two pedestal tables by the front windows. Sign-writing is raised serif capitals drawn as strokes (thick stems, thin hairlines, 45° bowl facets, slab serifs); crossing strokes stand a few millimetres apart in depth.
