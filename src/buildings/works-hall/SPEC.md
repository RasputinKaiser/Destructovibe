# Works hall

26 × 14 m, 6 m to eaves, 4 bays: `portalShed` with a half-open roller shutter in the south front and a steel door in the
west gable, a lighting tray along the inner flanges of the north columns (fed by a riser from the floor feeder through a
distribution board against the north wall), a wet-pipe sprinkler installation hung from the rafter-top plane on drop
rods cut to the roof slope (three ranges off a cross main, riser up the west gable from its control valve set), and
three steam unit heaters. The palette entry drops the grid stubs below floor level (the map calls `worksHall`
directly and keeps them). Footprint frame only; no datums.

Known gaps:
- No floor slab or joints (site pad).
- No office pod (piece budget).
- The shell's gaps (sheet gloss and crumpling, the stripped-sheet strips, member bending, GRP, ambient, the dust
  look and E8 box) are listed in portal-shed/SPEC.md. The gable rafter read curved (M-c): the gable frames are now
  unhaunched.
