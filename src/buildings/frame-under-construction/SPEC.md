# Steel frame under construction

15 × 9 m steel frame mid-erection, 2 × 1 bays (7.5 m by a 9 m span): six UC 254 columns on base plates with
holding-down bolts, two storeys tall with their heads left long for the next lift's splices; the first floor's UB 457
beams all in (bolted end plates with three bolt rows, bolts and nuts as detail — along X onto the column flanges, along
Z coped between the flanges onto the column webs, a secondary beam at each mid-bay coped onto the edge beams), the
first floor decked (one bay cast, one bare trapezoidal deck, ribs spanning between the Z beams) with a decking bundle
landed on the bare bay; the second floor's back-line and west beams in; temporary X-bracing (primed angles) in the
back line and the west gable (lapped onto the column flange faces); edge protection along the open first-floor front; the
next delivery (four UB 457s on timber bearers, a decking bundle) on the ground. Footprint frame only.

Known gaps:
- No MEWP or erectors (the map's scissor lift and mobile crane stand nearby).
- No shear studs or mesh on the bare deck.
- The deck soffit is tinted cool to offset the renderer's warm ground bounce (engine hook 7).
- All column heads stop 0.7 m above level 2 (the next lift's splice height).
- Members do not bend (engine hook 1).
