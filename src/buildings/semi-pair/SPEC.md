# Semi-detached pair

1930s semi pair under one hipped clay-tile roof, front +Z, halves mirrored; single part `main` (pipeline: raw). Brick ground storey (stretcher bond), rendered first storey, two-storey canted bays with a tile-hung panel, paired doors under a shared canopy, boxed eaves and gutters all round, a four-pot stack on the party wall. Per-half services (consumer unit, lamp, combi boiler, radiator, gas to an outside meter, sink stopcock).

Load paths: hip roof slabs seat on all four wall tops through a flat seat strip and meet on vertical planes through the hips and ridge; first floor pocketed into the party wall, butting the outer walls; bays bear on their brick bases and the front wall face.

Bay tile-hanging is ~165×100 mm plain clay tiles with 4 mm lap gaps; render is 0.15 m units with per-half tint and base darkening; floors are `timberDeck`.

The bay's tile-hanging hangs each tile on its nibs, leaning out 12 mm at the foot, so every course stands proud of the one below and casts a shadow line; tiles butt tight (no mortar joint) and are drawn as matte clay.
Known gaps (package): cavity wall modelled as solid brick with render units; the tiles are flat plates without camber or rounded corners; no stair or partitions; bay side lights are plain lites; the hall lamp glints through the tile-hanging joints of the bay panel.
Known gaps (engine; critic loop 1 round 4, not package-fixable): see cottage-row SPEC — carved load-bearing stubs, roof hang and plateau, see-through lattice clumps without mortar or fines, landed slabs that never break, black sprite smoke and emissive burning timber, hundreds of bodies still awake 60 s after an event. Map-owned: grid meter heads (M2), forecourts (M3).
Known gaps (engine, carried from critic loop 1 round 4 findings): timber floor and ceiling decks float or cantilever off a party wall 65-86 s after every event (N11; frozen or viewer-dependent collapse); in the game the four-pot flue stack still stands at 8 s after its wall's deletion while headless has it down by 2 s; a flat skin unit (render, plaster) draws its texture scaled to the cell, so a render cell repeats the same texture patch (the painted render reads as faint repeated blotches, no longer a tinted patchwork); cylinder units draw a chamfer at each of their 16 facets that edge-wear lightens into stripes, so pots are octagonal.
