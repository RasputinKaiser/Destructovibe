# Site cabin

20 ft steel container office (6 × 2.4 m) on timber sleepers with a window and a steel security door in its long side
and the container's cargo doors (two leaves, locking bars) at one end. One simulated body; its detail is the corner
posts, top and bottom rails, corrugated panels, cargo doors, roof sheet and floor. Footprint frame only.

Known gaps: the window is a plain pane; no steps, no interior.
