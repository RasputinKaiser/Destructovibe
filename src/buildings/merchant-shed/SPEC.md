# Builders merchant shed

20 × 12 m, 3 bays: `portalShed` in painted grey steel sheet, open-fronted across the two yard bays (a fascia over the
opening, the front columns exposed), the third bay closed, the lower band slate blue. Two runs of pallet racking along the back wall (blue frames,
orange beams at floor, 1.5 and 3.0 m, one body each) carrying their stock as detail: sawn timber packs, bagged cement,
concrete blocks, plasterboard; two stickered timber packs on bearers under cover; three boxes of stock; the office consumer unit on the
west gable with its lighting conduit run up the gable and along under the rafters' bottom flanges to a bay light.
Footprint frame only.

Known gaps:
- The racking is rigid with its stock (one body per run).
- The timber packs are stickered every fourth course with boards cut to random length, but each board is still a
  plain box (no end grain or sawn ends on the stretched wood texture).
- The lower wall band was brown and streaky (M-e): the default band (the wall grey darkened) read brown in the site's
  warm light under the gloss 'paint' finish; it is now slate blue. The streaks are the sheets' crowns
  and the gloss (see portal-shed/SPEC.md).
- The shell's gaps are listed in portal-shed/SPEC.md.
